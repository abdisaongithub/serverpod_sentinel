We are excited to announce the first release of **Serverpod Sentinel**!

Our mission is to empower developers and ops teams to own their monitoring stack. By combining the power of Serverpod's real-time communication with modern AI insights, Sentinel helps you resolve incidents faster than ever.

## Why Sentinel?

Incident management tools are often expensive, SaaS-only, and hard to customize. Sentinel is different. It's open-source, written in Dart from top to bottom, and designed to be hosted on your own infrastructure.

### What's coming next?

We are working on:
- Deeper LLM integrations for predictive health.
- More agent modules (Docker monitoring, CloudWatch integration).
- Mobile management app.

Stay tuned!
