# Serverpod Sentinel: Production Readiness Roadmap

This roadmap outlines the ambitious journey from a functional prototype to a global, enterprise-grade monitoring and incident management platform.

## 🚀 The Vision
To provide a unified, intelligent, and highly scalable "Sentinel" that empowers engineering teams to maintain 99.99% availability with minimal toil.

---

## 📅 Roadmap Overview

### [Phase I: Security, Identity & Compliance](./PHASE_1_SECURITY_IDENTITY_COMPLIANCE.md)
**Focus:** Building a rock-solid foundation of security and trust.
- MFA, SSO, and Granular RBAC.
- Encryption at rest and in transit (mTLS).
- Immutable audit trails and compliance reporting.

### [Phase II: Scalability & Resilience](./PHASE_2_SCALABILITY_RESILIENCE.md)
**Focus:** Ensuring the system can handle growth and infrastructure failures.
- Horizontal scaling and WebSocket load balancing.
- Database partitioning and read replicas.
- Multi-AZ disaster recovery.

### [Phase III: Intelligent Operations](./PHASE_3_INTELLIGENT_OPERATIONS.md)
**Focus:** Leveraging AI and deep observability to reduce MTTR (Mean Time To Resolve).
- AI-powered Root Cause Analysis and Predictive Anomalies.
- Visual Playbook Builder and auto-remediation.
- SLO/SLI management.

### [Phase IV: Ecosystem & Growth](./PHASE_4_ECOSYSTEM_GROWTH.md)
**Focus:** Extending the platform to integrate with the entire engineering toolkit.
- Multi-language SDKs and OpenTelemetry support.
- Public APIs, Webhooks, and Integration Marketplace.
- Bidirectional ChatOps (Slack/Teams).

### [Phase V: Operational Maturity](./PHASE_5_OPERATIONAL_MATURITY.md)
**Focus:** Global governance, self-healing systems, and long-term excellence.
- Multi-region deployment and Edge monitoring.
- Autonomous remediation and Chaos Engineering.
- Engineering Health and Sustainability tracking.

---

## 📈 Success Metrics
- **Availability:** 99.99% for the Sentinel platform itself.
- **Latency:** <100ms for real-time telemetry streaming.
- **Security:** Zero high/critical vulnerabilities in automated scans.
- **Adoption:** Seamless integration with at least 50+ third-party services.
- **Intelligence:** >80% accuracy in AI-generated Root Cause Analysis.
